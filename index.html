About Me.

Hello, thank you for taking your time to view my portfolio. Two main factors that led me wanting to create websites at this point in my life are 1. a class I took called "Web Design" and 2. my gorgeous fiancé. She is a HUGE advocate of clean living and wanted a blog to get her voice out there, so for a course project, I created a site for her... and here I am today!

My fiancé is by far the best thing that has happened to me. She's my world and my most significant on this journey support as I am hers. On this site, you will find the projects that I have worked on. Some projects that you will find are websites and videos I have made over time. Taking photos is also a hobby of mine, which most of the pictures that you would see on my site are photos that I have taken, especially the ones that are in my YouTube video.

Some hobbies of mine (besides the ones I have already mentioned) are hiking, fishing, camping, exploring different parts of the world (which is a favorite of mine), spending time with loved ones, and just enjoying life all-together. Life is too short, not to enjoy it. Take the time to spend with your loved ones and explore as much of the world as you can. That is how I try to live my life.
