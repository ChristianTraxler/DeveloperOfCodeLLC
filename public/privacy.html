Privacy Policy

Last updated October 12, 2026

Developer of Code, LLC ("we", "us") builds websites and software for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through developerofcode.com, our emails and our tools, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

  • Consultation and project requests. When you fill in a form on this site we receive what you enter, such as your name, email, phone number and project details.
  • Launch notifications. When you ask to hear about a product launch we receive your email address and the product you picked.
  • Email you send us. If you write to us, we keep the conversation so we can reply and follow up.

Information collected automatically

This site uses Google Tag Manager to measure visits, which may set cookies and collect standard technical details such as pages viewed, browser type and approximate location. Our marketing emails can record when a message is opened or a link is clicked, so we know what is useful.

How we use information

  • To answer your request, prepare quotes and deliver the work you hire us for.
  • To send the emails you asked for, such as a launch notice or project updates.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except the service providers that help us run the business (listed below), or when the law requires it.

Service providers

We use Vercel to host this site, Supabase to store data for our tools, Resend to send email, and Google for analytics and for our own business Gmail. Each processes information only to provide its service to us.

Our internal Mail tool and Google user data

Our private admin area includes a Mail tool that connects to our own business Gmail account through Google's Gmail API, so we can read, send and organize our company email in one place. It is not offered to the public, and only our signed-in staff can use it.

  • What it accesses. Email messages, labels, drafts and attachments in the linked Gmail account, using the gmail.modify permission. It cannot permanently delete mail.
  • How it is used. Only to show that mailbox to its owner and to carry out the actions they take, such as sending a reply, archiving or labeling.
  • Storage. Google's access tokens are encrypted before they are stored. Message contents are fetched from Gmail when viewed and are not kept in our database, apart from short-lived change notices that are cleared automatically.
  • Sharing. Gmail data is never sold, never used for advertising, never used to train AI models, and never shared with third parties.
  • Removing access. The account owner can unlink Gmail inside the tool at any time, or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Unlinking deletes the stored tokens.

Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link that takes effect right away.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Security and retention

We protect information with encrypted connections, access controls and encryption of sensitive credentials. We keep information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires, then delete it.

Children

Our site and services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
