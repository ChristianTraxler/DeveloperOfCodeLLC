FAQ

Common questions.

Do I really own the code?
Yes. With the License or Hosted Setup, you get the full source code and a perpetual license to use it for your business. You can modify it, deploy it wherever you want, and you'll never lose access — even if Developer Of Code closes up shop tomorrow.
What's the difference between License and Hosted Setup?
License is for technical folks who want to deploy it themselves — you get the code, a guide, and 30 days of email support. Hosted Setup is for everyone else — I do the full deployment for you, connect the database, apply your own branding, and walk you through using it.
Is the maintenance retainer required?
No. The CRM works without it. The retainer covers security updates, bug fixes, and new features as I ship them — basically peace of mind that your tool keeps getting better. It's recommended for the Hosted Setup since I'm already managing your hosting, but it's optional for everyone.
Why no monthly subscription option?
Honestly? Because subscription fatigue is real, and the existing CRM market is already saturated with $15–$50/month options. Relay Solo's whole pitch is: pay once, own it, never pay another monthly fee just to access your own contact list. The optional maintenance retainer is the only recurring charge, and it covers active development — not access to the app itself.
Can I import data from another CRM?
CSV import is on the roadmap. If you're on the Hosted Setup, I can do a one-time data migration from your existing CRM as part of onboarding — just include a sample export when you reach out.
What happens if I outgrow it?
Relay Solo is built for one person. If you grow to a team, the full Relay CRM (multi-user, with team permissions, shared inboxes, and more) is also available from Developer Of Code — and you'll get a credit toward upgrading.
Refund policy?
14-day money-back guarantee on the License and Hosted Setup. The maintenance retainer can be cancelled any time with no further charges.
